about_me

Student, engineer, and
robotics developer.

I'm a first-generation student from Pittsburgh, PA with a passion for technology that started early — and hasn't slowed down since.

At age 7 I was already setting up Xbox consoles, and by 13 I taught myself to build computers through YouTube tutorials. That same year I got my first Arduino and began exploring C++.

At 14 I dove into Linux using a Raspberry Pi and Ubuntu, fueling my interest in automation and programming. Eager to challenge myself, I skipped the introductory Python class at school and advanced to the next level. Around that time I joined an FTC robotics team, stepped up as head programmer, and learned Java on the fly to help lead the team to a competitive robot.

Now at 16 I code in multiple languages and work on robotics, automation, and software development — constantly seeking new opportunities to learn, build, and innovate. Last summer I attended Pittsburgh Cyber Camp for hands-on experience with cybersecurity.
