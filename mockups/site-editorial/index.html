About

I'm a first-generation student from Pittsburgh with a passion for technology that started early — and hasn't slowed down since.

At age 7 I was setting up Xbox consoles; by 13 I taught myself to build computers from YouTube and got my first Arduino, exploring C++. At 14 I moved to Linux on a Raspberry Pi, skipped the intro Python class at school, and joined an FTC robotics team — where I stepped up as head programmer and learned Java on the fly to help lead the team to a competitive robot.

Now at 16 I work across robotics, automation, and software, always looking for the next thing to build. Last summer I attended Pittsburgh Cyber Camp for hands-on cybersecurity.
